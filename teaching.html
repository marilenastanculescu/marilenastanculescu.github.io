Doctoral supervision

Habilitation in Electrical Engineering (certificate 5365 of 7 October 2021). PhD supervisor in the Doctoral School of Electrical Engineering, POLITEHNICA Bucharest, since October 2021 (decision 91/18.10.2021). In 2025, six doctoral students in funded positions were under supervision, and she served on doctoral supervisory committees for further theses.

Diploma and dissertation projects

Supervision of bachelor’s and master’s projects, including master’s dissertations in Electrical Systems, Intelligent Instrumentation and Measurement Systems, and Advanced Electrical Systems.

Academic committees

Chair of the bachelor’s thesis examination committee for Applied Informatics in Electrical Engineering (2025); member of master’s committees and of bachelor’s admission committees (2025). Member of the Faculty of Electrical Engineering promotion team at Polifest 2025 and of the Marcel Roșculeț competition committee.
