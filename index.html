Research interests

From field theory to application.

01 / FIELDS

Electromagnetic field modelling

Numerical methods (FEM, BEM, hybrid iterative methods), eddy currents and non-destructive magnetic flaw detection.

Research areas →
02 / WIRELESS

Wireless power transfer

Inductive and magnetically coupled systems, S-parameters and efficiency, contactless charging of electric vehicles.

Research areas →
03 / CIRCUITS

Circuits & power quality

Analysis of analog and nonlinear circuits, the Smith chart, harmonics and power quality in modern networks.
