About me

My work focuses on the modelling of electric and magnetic field problems, the wireless transmission of electromagnetic energy and the development of numerical methods for electromagnetic field computation. At the Faculty of Electrical Engineering of POLITEHNICA Bucharest, I teach the fundamentals of electrical engineering in English and Romanian, supervise doctoral students and work with colleagues on national and European research projects.
