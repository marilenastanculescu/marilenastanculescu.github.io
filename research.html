Areas of scientific expertise

Modelling of electric and magnetic field problems; wireless transmission of electromagnetic energy; development of numerical methods for solving electromagnetic field problems. Recent work also covers power quality and harmonics, sensitivity analysis in non-sinusoidal regimes, predictive maintenance with artificial intelligence and exposure to electromagnetic fields.
